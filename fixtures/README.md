# Fixtures

Hand-written pastes used as conformance cases. They **name** game entities and nothing else
(content boundary, CLAUDE.md). `vice` and `shigen` are Francesco's two real builds (D17); the
compiled sheets they mirror live outside this repo.

Known fidelity notes:
- `vice`: the paper sheet lists three invocations at level 1; a 2024 Warlock has one at level 1
  and three at level 2, so the paste places two of them at `L2`. Skills chosen at Warlock 1 are
  not recorded on the compiled sheet, so the line is omitted (D13).
- `shigen`: the Human origin feat is written as `Feat:` at `L1` (§5.1: any feat slot). Skills
  chosen at Fighter 1 are not recorded on the compiled sheet and are omitted. Class skills of the
  homebrew background are granted, not chosen, so they do not appear.
